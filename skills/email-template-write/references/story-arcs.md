# Story arcs

Pick **one** arc, then a length tier. The arc fixes the section order between header and footer: do not reorder, but drop sections the content cannot support. The names describe section types; build them from the section snippets and the design rules.

## Choosing the arc

| Arc | Use when |
|---|---|
| `flash_sale` | Discount, promo code, % off, limited time, clearance. Pushes immediate purchase. |
| `new_launch` | A specific new product, collection or feature, pre-order, "just arrived". |
| `founders_letter` | Brand story, mission, milestone, anniversary. Relationship, nothing to sell. |
| `curated_digest` | Newsletter, roundup, weekly picks, several topics, no single hero product. |
| `win_back` | Inactive or lapsed contacts: "we miss you", "what's changed". |
| `social_proof` | Reviews, testimonials, ratings, press. Audience needs trust before buying. |
| `teacher` | How-to, guide, tips, problem/solution, workshops, classes, free-to-paid upgrades. |
| `vip_moment` | Loyalty, rewards, early access, members only, "you've earned it". |
| `seasonal_greeting` | Holiday or cultural moment (Christmas, Valentine's, back to school), with or without an offer. |
| `personal_outreach` | Welcome, onboarding, follow-up, feedback, survey, thank you. One-to-one tone. |
| `second_chance` | Abandoned cart, items left behind, back in stock. |

Mixed signals: keep the first arc's structure and borrow from the second (launch + seasonal: launch with gift-guide framing; win-back + VIP: win-back with an exclusive reward).

## Length tiers

Counted without header and footer.

| Tier | Sections | Use for |
|---|---|---|
| short | 2 to 3 | One clear action, time-sensitive, cart recovery, simple re-engagement, brief follow-up |
| medium | 4 to 5 | **Default.** Standard promotion, launch, newsletter, workshop promotion |
| full | 6 to 8 | Educational guide, multi-item digest, detailed brand story, catalogue |

- An explicit request always wins: "short", "brief", "quick", "reminder" mean short; "detailed", "complete", "in-depth", "full guide" mean full.
- Otherwise arc defaults: `win_back`, `second_chance`, `personal_outreach` are short; `curated_digest`, `teacher` are full; all others are medium.
- The recipes below often list more sections than the tier count. Keep the order and drop the weakest ones.

## Medium sequences

**flash_sale**: hero-full-width (the deal), price-lockup-badge, promo-code-coupon, image-text-split (product), image-grid-2col, stats-numbers-row (trust), eyebrow-headline-body (closing call to action).
Full adds features, testimonial, cross-sell.

**new_launch**: eyebrow-headline-body (teaser), hero-full-width (reveal), image-text-split (alternating-image-text for 2+ products), feature-grid-2x2, image-grid-2col, price-lockup-badge (price and button).
Short drops the teaser and split. Full adds a testimonial and cross-sell.

**founders_letter**: left-border-pull-quote (opening hook), image-text-split (the story), centered-statement (the point), eyebrow-headline-body (the ask), circular-avatar-portrait (signature).
Short: story, statement, signature. Full adds milestone stats.

**curated_digest**: hero-full-width (newsletter title), eyebrow-headline-body (editor's note), image-text-split (main feature), image-grid-2x2, alternating-image-text (secondary items), left-border-pull-quote (quick hits), centered-statement (community prompt).
Full adds a 3-column grid, sponsored slot, recommended reads.

**win_back**: hero-full-width, centered-statement (warm hook), feature-grid-2x2 (what's new), testimonial-quote, promo-code-coupon (the offer), product-showcase-side-by-side (top products).
Short: first three plus coupon. Full adds a gallery and cross-sell.

**social_proof**: testimonial-quote, image-text-split (product and rating), stats-numbers-row, image-grid-2col (visual proof), centered-statement (bridge to buying), testimonial-quote (second).
Full adds press mentions and a 3-column proof grid.

**teacher**: eyebrow-headline-body (problem), left-border-pull-quote (why it happens), image-text-split (solution reveal), step-by-step-process (how it works), feature-grid-2x2 (benefits).
Short: problem, solution, steps. Full adds a testimonial.

**vip_moment**: eyebrow-headline-body (personal greeting), stats-numbers-row (recognition), product-showcase-side-by-side (reward), price-lockup-badge (exclusive reward), promo-code-coupon (how to claim), feature-grid-2x2 (what's unlocked).
Full adds a pull quote and testimonial.

**seasonal_greeting**: hero-full-width (seasonal), image-text-split (brand and season), product-showcase-side-by-side (gift guide), promo-code-coupon, centered-statement (warm close).
Short drops the brand split. Full adds a gift-card nudge.

**personal_outreach**: eyebrow-headline-body (context and purpose), centered-statement (the one ask).
Short: one text section with one button. Full adds a pull quote and avatar sign-off.

**second_chance**: hero-text-on-color (friendly reminder), eyebrow-headline-body (gentle hook), cart-line-items, left-border-pull-quote (answer the objection), testimonial-quote (product review), icon-strip-horizontal (shipping, secure payment).
Short: reminder, cart, trust strip. Full adds cross-sell.

## Key anti-patterns

| Arc | Avoid |
|---|---|
| flash_sale | Discount below the fold; more than one promo code block; long story before the offer; no deadline |
| new_launch | Opening with text instead of a striking product visual; leading with price |
| founders_letter | Product grids, product hero, promo codes; corporate phrasing ("We are pleased to announce") |
| curated_digest | One hero dominating the top; single-column list; items without their own link |
| win_back | Aggressive urgency; too many products; no incentive; discount with no "what's new" |
| social_proof | Unattributed quotes; stats without context; text-only proof; only perfect 5-star reviews |
| teacher | Wall of text; pitching the product before delivering value; unnumbered steps |
| vip_moment | Generic greeting; light airy design (use a premium dark/bold look); hiding the reward |
| seasonal_greeting | Hard sell before the greeting; generic stock imagery; offer-first structure |
| second_chance | Guilt-tripping; no product images; no reviews; fake urgency |
| personal_outreach | Any images or heavy visuals; corporate tone; more than **one** button; no first-name personalisation |

Also, across all arcs: for a discount-led brief, move the price or coupon right after the hero. Never more than 2 text-only sections in a row. An image-text split comes in mirrored pairs (image left, then image right); for a single item use a full-width section.
